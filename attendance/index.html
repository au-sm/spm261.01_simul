Attendance & Ticket Sales

SPM261.01 Soccer League · set your price tier every home match — attendance responds to price, form, and Star Power

How It Works

Every home match, you choose a ticket price tier on the Weekly Lineup form. Revenue = attendance × price — the classic price-elasticity tradeoff: Premium pays more per fan but empties seats; Budget fills the building but caps the per-ticket upside. Stadium capacity is 20,000.

TierPriceAttendance Effect
Budget$15+15 pts
Standard$30+0 pts
Premium$50-30 pts

The full formula, starting from a 60% base fill rate:

Priceset by your tier above — the only thing you directly control
Recent Formwin rate over your last 3 matches, ±20 points of attendance at the extremes (0.5 = neutral, no history yet)
Star Poweryour STARTING XI's average Star Power (not full roster — fans come to see who's actually playing), ±7 points of attendance at the extremes

These three add together on top of the 60% base, then the result is clamped between 5% and 100% before being applied to capacity. The floor is deliberately low: Premium pricing on a team with no form and no Star Power is allowed to crater — a generous floor would quietly rescue Premium and erase the whole price-elasticity lesson.

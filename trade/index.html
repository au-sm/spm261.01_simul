Trade Center

SPM261.01 Soccer League · propose a trade, respond to one sent to you, deadline before Round 7

How It Works

Deadline. Trades must be fully agreed to (both teams accepted) before Round 7 -- Round 6 is the last round a trade can still land. No trades take effect in Rounds 7-17 or at any point after -- this prevents last-minute roster stacking right before the playoff-qualification picture is set, the same reason real leagues carry a trade deadline.

How many players. Every trade must send the SAME number of players back each way -- 1-for-1, 2-for-2, or 3-for-3 only, never an uneven count like 1-for-2. On top of that, a team may trade away at most 3 players total, combining every trade it makes, before the Round 6 deadline -- once a team has moved 3 players, it's done trading for the season, win or lose on those swaps.

Voluntary. Trading is entirely optional. No team is required to make a trade, and nobody has to accept an offer just because it's sent -- if the two sides can't agree on fair value, the simplest right answer is to not trade.

Salary cap. A trade is only valid if BOTH teams remain at or under salary_cap (see league_config.json) after the trade completes.

Cash considerations. Either side may add cash (from its budget, not a salary-cap item) on top of the player swap to make up for a value gap -- paying extra cash to land a clearly better player than what you're giving up is a normal, allowed trade structure, the same way real clubs attach cash to a deal.

Approval. A trade only takes effect once BOTH teams agree to it: the proposing team submits a Trade Proposal, and it sits pending until the receiving team accepts or declines it on the Trade Center page. Accepting applies it immediately (once it clears the roster/cap checks below); declining ends it with no changes to either roster.

Trade Day. One in-class Trade Day, scheduled mid-season (see the Season Calendar) as the suggested window to negotiate face to face -- not a forced transaction. Every team is paired with exactly one partner from a published rotation (engine/generate_trade_day_rotation.py, same no-repeat pairing method as the Sponsorship Negotiation rotation) to talk through a possible trade that class period; an odd team count leaves one team unpaired, and the instructor fills in as that team's partner, the same convention already used for the Sponsorship Negotiation rotation's bye. Whether that conversation turns into an actual submitted Trade Proposal is up to the two teams -- Trade Day is a floor for negotiation, not a cap: the ordinary voluntary trade window stays open before and after it, through Round 6.

How it works

  • Your market tier sets the rep's opening offer: a yearly rate and a performance clause. You can sign it straight away, which is guaranteed and risk-free.
  • Or negotiate. Every offer you make needs a pitch: why should the network pay more for your club? Real numbers from your season (standings, attendance, star power) persuade the rep; vague claims don't.
  • How far the network will go depends on your Negotiating Leverage (Rulebook Section 11). A weak hand can end below the opening offer once you start negotiating.
  • You get 6 offers, then the rep makes a final offer. Asking far more than the network will ever pay counts as a strike. After 3 strikes the rep walks away until tomorrow.
  • A looser performance clause is a separate ask. One step looser than your opening clause needs leverage 20+, two steps 50+, and strict to no clause 80+. Asking never costs you revenue.
  • You sign once, and the deal is final. AI-negotiated deals earn no commission. Every conversation is saved for your instructor.
  • National TV has nothing to negotiate. National and local TV money post to your budget together on TV Revenue Day, after your instructor closes negotiations. No signed deal by then means your base rate and base clause.

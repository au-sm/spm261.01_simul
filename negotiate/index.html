How it works

  • Each brand opens with a public offer: a yearly revenue and a performance clause. You can sign it straight away, which is guaranteed and risk-free.
  • Or negotiate. Every offer you make needs a pitch: why should this brand pay more for your club? Real numbers from your season (standings, attendance, star power) persuade a rep; vague claims don't.
  • How far a brand will go depends on your Negotiating Leverage (Rulebook Section 11). A weak hand can end below the opening offer once you start negotiating.
  • You get 6 offers per brand, then the rep makes a final offer. Asking far more than a brand will ever pay counts as a strike. After 3 strikes the rep walks away, and that brand is closed to your club for the day.
  • A looser clause is a separate ask (Modest, Bold, Very Bold). It only works if your leverage supports it, and asking never costs you revenue.
  • One brand per category, 6 categories required. AI-negotiated deals earn no sponsor-rep commission. Every conversation is saved for your instructor.

Rules

  • The Health Hub opens for Round 3. A treatment always applies to the next round to be played.
  • Up to 4 treatments per team per round, and only one treatment per player per round.
  • Rehab is for injured players; Recovery and Sports-Science are for healthy players. Condition can't rise above Excellent.
  • The cost is deducted from your team's cash on the Budget Dashboard. It does not reduce the Revenue component of your Season Scorecard.
  • Requests are processed about every 30 minutes. Check the Dashboard's Player Condition report before you submit your lineup to confirm the treatment took effect.
  • A request that fails a check (not enough cash, round already played, limit reached) is voided and listed in the log below with the reason. Voided requests cost nothing.
  • A treatment is a business decision: explain it in that round's lineup rationale, where it is scored under Business/Financial Reasoning.

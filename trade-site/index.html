Trade Center

SPM261.01 Soccer League · propose a trade, respond to one sent to you, two trade sessions, 3 players out and 3 in each

Trade Session 1 is OPEN. Every team must move 3 players out and 3 in. It closes when Round 7 is simulated.

How It Works

Deadline. There are two trade sessions this season. Trade Session 1 is open now and closes when Round 7 is simulated -- a trade must be accepted by both teams before then. Trade Session 2 will open later in the season; its date will be announced in class and on this page. Outside an open session, no trade can take effect -- this prevents last-minute roster stacking, the same reason real leagues carry a trade deadline.

How many players. Each trade session, every team must move exactly 3 players out and bring exactly 3 players in -- no more, no less, so rosters always stay at 18. Incoming players can come from another team or from the free-agent pool (the undrafted players on the Draft Board). In any deal, the players you swap with a partner team must match one-for-one (1-for-1, 2-for-2 or 3-for-3). For each free agent you sign, you release one of your own players to the free-agent pool. Example: send 2 players to a partner for 2 of theirs, and release 1 player to sign 1 free agent. Your 3 can be one deal or several, but never more than 3 in a session. A partner in a 2-for-2 has only moved 2, so it still owes 1 more move that session.

Mandatory. Trading is mandatory. By the end of each trade session every team must have moved its 3 players. You choose your partner and the terms, and nobody has to accept a particular offer -- if a deal isn't fair value, decline it and work out a better one, or use the free-agent pool instead. If two teams claim the same free agent, the lower-ranked team gets him (see Free-agent priority).

Salary cap. A trade is only valid if BOTH teams remain at or under salary_cap (see league_config.json) after the trade completes.

Cash considerations. Either side may add cash (from its budget, not a salary-cap item) on top of the player swap to make up for a value gap -- paying extra cash to land a clearly better player than what you're giving up is a normal, allowed trade structure, the same way real clubs attach cash to a deal. Cash only applies to deals with a partner team, not to free-agent moves.

Approval. A deal with another team only takes effect once BOTH teams agree to it: the proposing team submits a Trade Proposal, and it sits pending until the receiving team accepts or declines it on the Trade Center page. Accepting applies it at the next league-office check (once it clears the roster/cap checks below); declining ends it with no changes to either roster. A free-agent-only move needs no one's approval, but like every free-agent signing it waits for waivers.

Free-agent priority. Once a team claims a free agent, he is removed from the free-agent list, so no other team can pick him. Any deal that signs a free agent waits as a claim until the instructor processes waivers. Then every waiting claim is applied in reverse standings order, worst record first. If two claims for the same player arrive at the same moment, the lower-ranked team gets him and the other claim is voided, so that team should submit a different move. A voided claim puts the player back on the free-agent list. Waiting claims are also processed right before the next round is simulated. Deals that don't involve free agents apply at the next league-office check as usual.

Trade Day. Each trade session includes an in-class Trade Day. Every owner must meet at least 3 other teams. Class starts with 3 short rotations: owners sit in two facing rows, meet one owner for 5 minutes, then rotate seats, so everyone meets 3 different teams. Use each meeting to find leads: what you would move, what positions you need, and your salary room. After the rotations, the room opens up: go back to your best leads or talk to any other team, agree on deals and submit them on this page before class ends. Your 3 players can be moved with any team or the free-agent pool, and the session stays open until it closes.

Propose a Trade

One proposal = one deal with one partner. To trade with two or three teams (for example, three 1-for-1 trades), send a separate proposal to each team -- the form clears after each one. Each session your team moves exactly 3 players out and 3 in, in one deal or several. Players you swap with a partner must match one-for-one; for the rest, release a player (set it to "release to free agents") and sign a free agent. Pick "No partner -- free agents only" for a free-agent-only move, which needs no one's approval. Cash is only for deals with a partner team.

TV Rights Marketplace

SPM261.01 Soccer League · every team negotiates ONE Local TV Deal rate, right after Draft Day

How It Works

Unlike Sponsorship, there's no brand catalog here — your Local TV Deal is tied to a market tier randomly assigned right after Draft Day (evenly split league-wide, drawn from the season seed). That tier sets your PUBLIC base rate:

Market TierBase Rate
Major Market$1,200,000
Mid Market$700,000
Small Market$500,000

Right after that assignment, you negotiate LIVE against a classmate playing the network rep (rotation: engine/generate_tv_negotiation_rotation.py). Same revenue mechanic as Sponsorship — the ONLY axis here, no clause:

RevenueTake the market-tier Base exactly (guaranteed), or Negotiate — lands anywhere from −10% to +10% of base, set by your Negotiating Leverage (roster avg. Star Power + a standings bonus once the season starts). A weak hand can genuinely land below base.
The commissionLand ABOVE base, and the overage is credited — immediately, not at the Round 3 split — as bonus revenue to whichever team played the network rep. Landing at or below base costs the rep's team nothing, but earns them nothing either.

This negotiation sets the RATE only. The money itself is still paid at the league's Round 3 TV Revenue Day, with the Star Power modifier (+15% if your starting XI is top-3 league-wide then) still applied on top of whatever rate you locked in here.

The League-Wide (National) TV Deal is separate and fully automatic — a shared formula-only split (base + standings/Star Power bonus) with no live negotiation, since there's no natural second party to negotiate a league-wide number against. See the Rulebook, Section 9.

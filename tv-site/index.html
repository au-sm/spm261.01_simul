SPM261.01 Soccer League · every team negotiates ONE Local TV Deal

How It Works

Unlike Sponsorship, there's no brand catalog here — your Local TV Deal is tied to a market tier randomly assigned right after Draft Day (evenly split across all five tiers league-wide, drawn from the season seed). That tier sets your base clause:

Market TierBase Clause
Major Marketstrict
Large Marketstrict
Mid Marketstandard
Small Marketstandard
Micro Marketloose

Your market tier's actual base rate is not posted publicly anywhere on this site. The rep opens with it when you sit down to negotiate — the same convention Sponsorship uses.

You negotiate one-on-one on the AI TV Rep page. Two separate things are on the table:

RevenueTake the market-tier Base exactly (guaranteed), or Negotiate — lands anywhere from −10% to +10% of base, set by your Negotiating Leverage (roster avg. Star Power + a standings bonus once the season starts). A weak hand can genuinely land below base.
ClauseA separate ask — one step looser than your base clause needs leverage 20+, two steps 50+, strict to no clause 80+. Asking never costs you revenue.
Limits6 offers, then a final offer. Asking far more than the network will pay is a strike; 3 strikes and the rep walks away for the day. AI deals earn no commission.

This negotiation sets the RATE and the CLAUSE. The money is paid on one TV Revenue Day, together with the League-Wide (National) TV Deal, once your instructor closes Local TV negotiations. The Star Power modifier (+15% if your latest starting XI is top-3 league-wide that day) applies on top of your rate. A team with no signed deal by the deadline gets its base rate and base clause. The clause is checked once at season end.

The National TV Deal is automatic — $800,000 for every team, plus a standings bonus for the top 3 and $500,000 for the top-3 starting XIs by Star Power, with nothing to negotiate. See the Rulebook's TV / Broadcast Revenue section.

      Playoff Qualification

      top 6 at season's end · no bracket games — see Standings above

      No playoff bracket is played. The top 6 teams in the final regular-season standings are recognized as Playoff Qualifiers and each receive a flat $400K Qualification Bonus — not tiered by seed, since finishing 1st vs. 6th is already rewarded by the standings themselves. The qualification line is marked directly in the Standings table above.

      TV / Broadcast Split

      Base: $800K/team

      Top team at split time: +1,000,000. 2nd: +600,000. 3rd: +300,000. Bottom 3 teams: +0 (no penalty beyond missing the bonus).

      If a team's average starting-XI Star Power ranks in the league's top 3 at split time, add +500,000 (broadcasters pay for star wattage independent of the standings) -- this can stack with the standings bonus.

      Split at: After round 3 of the 17-round regular season (18 teams, round-robin) -- adjust proportionally if team count changes.
